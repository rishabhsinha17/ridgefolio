Geographic base
NASA Blue Marble Next Generation with Topography and Bathymetry. NASA GIBS supplied the geographic imagery. The 89°E–144°E, 15°S–14°N region was rendered at 6,600 × 3,480 pixels. Ridgefolio applied a light map palette to the source relief. The local detail map uses vector geography when the display exceeds the native sampling of the geographic image.
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/
https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg4326/best/wms.cgi
