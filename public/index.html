Engineering you can inspect.

The University of Maryland research team published maps, charts and downloadable data about industrial parks in Indonesia. Rishabh built the dashboard as a research assistant at CGS.

Website engineering · University of Maryland

The archived May 2025 dataset contains 141 named industrial parks. Readers can explore the location and status of each one on the case-study map.

CGS handover dataset · May 2025

Nine Plotly charts turn the team’s CSV records into views of park status, electricity supply and industrial activity.

CGS dashboard · Chart implementation

Rishabh wrote a Python script that turns the master spreadsheet into seven geographic files. Each file keeps the same parks and selects the fields needed for its map layer.

CGS dashboard · Publishing workflow

The June 2024 launch presentation introduced a dataset of 140 parks and showed how to read six map layers.
