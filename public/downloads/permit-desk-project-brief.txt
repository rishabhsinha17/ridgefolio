RIDGEFOLIO | EXAMPLE PROJECT BRIEF
Community event permit review

Illustrative scope based on the working permit desk demonstration
https://ridgefolio.com/demos/permit-desk/

Current task
A resident submits an event name and location. A staff reviewer may need a more precise location before they can approve the request.

Desired result
The reviewer can request a correction. The resident sees the reason, updates the request and resubmits it. Both parties can follow the decision history.

Systems and constraints
The demonstration uses fictional records in a browser tab. A production project would require the agency to identify the system of record, define access and retention rules, and agree on notification delivery. Fees, payments and identity verification would require separate scoping.

Acceptance walkthrough
1. Submit "Neighborhood art fair" at "Harbor Square".
2. Request the specific entrance as the reviewer.
3. Change the location to "Harbor Square, east entrance" and resubmit.
4. Approve the application and download its receipt.
5. Check the event, corrected location, review note and approval against the case history.

Handover
Deliver the agreed field definitions, review rules, test records and staff walkthrough. Record which person at the agency accepts the result and owns ongoing changes.
