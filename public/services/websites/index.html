Resident experience

Websites & service portals

Help residents find a service, understand what they need and finish the task on a phone.

The starting point

Someone applying for a permit may have to piece together a department page, a PDF and a conversation by phone. Start by putting the instructions in one place.

Try the software

What the work includes

Service design
Map resident questions to plain-language pages. Test the navigation against tasks such as renewing a license or requesting a record.
Content migration
Inventory pages and documents, assign owners, map redirects and rehearse the cutover on a staging site.
Editorial handover
Give editors reusable page patterns and a publishing checklist. Walk an editor through a change before launch.

How to evaluate it

Ask a first-time visitor to locate the right service, check eligibility and open the application using only a keyboard. Ask an editor to update that same page.

Try the demonstration

What affects scope

To estimate the work, count pages and documents, identify required languages and check the current CMS. Include integrations, editor permissions and the launch date.
